Method

What the app is measuring

Welch PSD

Audio is divided into overlapping Hann-windowed segments. Their one-sided power spectra are averaged to reduce variance.

Power-law fit

The app regresses log10(power) on log10(frequency). The reported spectral exponent is β = −slope. Fit residual is evaluated in dB.

Conservative label

A color label is only treated as reliable when the spectrum is reasonably described by one power-law slope over the selected range.

Measurement note: the built-in iPhone microphone and iOS audio processing are not a calibrated acoustic measurement chain. This is suitable for exploratory/relative spectral analysis. For rigorous acoustic work, use a calibrated external microphone and known frequency-response correction.

NoiseColor estimates spectral shape, not perceived loudness or sound-pressure level.
